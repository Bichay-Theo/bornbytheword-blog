Osotua Musana o irorei le Esharunoto 1

Ore te nikisom Osotua Ng'ejuk nikinepu ilapolosak eiroro tialo olmusalaba too rorei anaa "Esharunoto", "Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie", "Esupatisho e Enkai tiatua enkoisiayio", meishiaakino nikirikino esipata sapuk: ilYaudia apa kulo apolosak. Etoiputukita apa iltauja lenye ndamunot e Osotua Musana o nkitanapat enyena, naa ina apa eyieu Enkai metaasi; amu eikitoibiro Enkai te Osotua Musana too rorei lenye o nkitanyaanyukot enyena o nkitanapat enyena endamunoto e tung'ani pee eng'amu Olsharunoni olotu. Te nkaraki ina, tenaa kiyieu kiyiolou esipa enatejo Paulo o Petero te nena kata apa pee eiro tialo "Esharunoto", mikindim aaisilig nembukunot e nkutuk e Yunani ake, kake meishiaakino nikirrinyokino ntoneta e kulo rorei tiatua Osotua Musana.

Tiatua embuku enye "Elikioroto e Ororei le Nkai e nkotunoto lo lmusalaba" (The Apostolic Preaching of the Cross), nayae anaa embuku kitok tiatua nkisomata e Esharunoto, keitigila oloiboni le Nkai "Leon Morris" nchere mme intanyaanyukot ake e nkisomata irorei le Enjeun, kake irorei oosipa le nkiguana o le Nkai ooyau te mpolos enye esipata e "olekoisiayio loo ng'ok lolakieki" te "olonyikoki lo sotua".

"Olsharunoni" (Go'el) o enkitanapata e nkotunoto 2

Kekenakini ororei le Lbrania (Go'el) anaa ororei kitok tiatua Osotua Musana, naitobirari te nkumoi anaa "Olsharunoni" aashu "Oloitajeunoni". Keirubare ele rorei oleng o "enkitanapata e nkotunoto". Tiatua enkitanapata e Osotua Musana (Ilawi 25:25) kikisom: «Tenaa ketotii aisinani olalashe lino nemir ntokitin enyena, kelotu olalashe lenye ontaaniki oleng neikordu ina naitamirakie olalashe lenye». Tenaa keaku Siraeli olaisinani nemir enkop enye aashu emir kewan anaa osinka pee elaku esile enye, kenya ninye enkotunoto enye katukul nemeidim aitajeu kewan, naa teine elotie esiaai e Olsharunoni pee eas "Esharunoto".

Ore Olsharunoni naa ilo kitok le sotua lo sarge, ore esiaai enye kitok naa alotu aitajeu olalashe lenye, kake meetae ena sharunoto nemeata esile. Kewang enkitanapata e Ororei le Nkai ake: pee esharu Olsharunoni olalashe lenye, meishiaakino nelotu ninye dukuya aitalaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok pooki te rishata enye. Meetae enkotunoto te nyieuata ake, nemeetae enkotunoto te nkaraki Olng'ur le Enkai ake; keyieu Esharunoto olekoisiayio loo ng'ok nitalakua olonyikoki le dukuya.

Ore enkitanyaanyukoto kitok naishu te ena naa "Boas" tiatua embuku e Ruti, ilo leitu eidim asharu enjung'ore e Elimelek neyam Ruti te nkaraki e nyorrata enye te ninye ake, kake eishiaakino ninye atalaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok te dukuya pooki neinyang'u esipata e Esharunoto te nkiguana: «Nejo Olsharunoni: Maidim nanu aasharu te nkaraki kewan ai pee maidany enjung'ore ai. Tasharu iyie kewan ena sharunoto ai amu maidim nanu asharu» (Ruti 4:6).

"Esharunoto" (Padhah) o eshekeli e Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie.. aishori ng'ae olekoisiayio loo ng'ok? 3

Ore olikae rorei le Lbrania naa (Padhah), oasishoreki aaitodol enkotunoto oo isinkan ntalakieki o elakunoto e esharunoto oo lkikaunot anaa enaitanapa Olaitoriani tiatua (Ena 13:13-15): «Naa ore pooki kikau loo layiok linono naa isharu iyie... amu keara Olaitoriani pooki kikau tiatua enkop e Misiri, eing'uaa olkikau lo ltung'ani o metabaiki olkikau e ng'ues. Te nkaraki ina apolosaki Olaitoriani... nasharu nanu pooki kikau loo layiok laainei». Keitasheyie ele rorei enkitanapata nagol: mme "enkotunoto" ake Esharunoto tiatua Ororei le Nkai ake, kake enkotunoto eimunye elakunoto e Esharunoto.

Kake, eishori ng'ae ena sharunoto? Keng'eno ewalata tiatua enkitanapata e "eshekeli e Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie" (Ena 30:11-16). Ina kata eitanapaki ilayiok le Israel meishoru esharunoto te nkaraki ntauja lenye pee aisik enkeeya te nkata e nkikena e sirit, netejo enkitanapata: «Keishoru pooki tung'ani esharunoto e nkishui enye to Laitoriani... ninchooyoyo enkishooroto o Laitoriani e Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie te nkaraki ntauja linyi». Kesapuk ele rorei oleng te lalahe e Enkai; meishori Esharunoto olaitoriani oitarruoisho nemeishori Olasurai (anaa enatejo kulikae tung'ana ng'eni le siadi te ng'oki), kake eishori Enkai makewan, anaa Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie te nkaraki Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok o Esharunoto te nkaraki enkishui pee eitu Ejeunoto e Enkiguenata sapuk e Enkai. Mikinepu tiatua Osotua Musana pooki embae e Esharunoto nasipa naitaasi eitu elakieki olekoisiayio loo ng'ok aashu eishori olonyikoki (anaa tenaa eshekeli ilo ekoisiayio loo ng'ok, aashu enkishui e olasar eibukokini anaa olonyikoki laaitiship Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok).

Ilarikok le eSírít e Kristo: Yesu Kristo ake anaa Olsharunoni nasipa 4

Ore ele rorei le Ororei le Nkai ake: eishiaakino neetae Olsharunoni o olalashe to sesen pee elaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok, mme enkitobirunoto e ng'ues aashu ndamunoto ng'ejuk nemeeyiolo eSírít e Kristo oo loo papaai, kake ninye apa entoneta nashetaki ilarikok le eSírít e Kristo e dukuya te nkilepunoto e nkolong eyiolounoto enye e nkitotio o lmusalaba.

Ore Olsinyati Atanasius Olarikoni, tiatua embuku enye ntarasi "Enkitotio e Ororei", eyiolou katukul nchere meidim Enkai aing'ar Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok nedany endung'okinoto e keeya te nkaraki Olng'ur le Enkai ake, anaa enemeidim oltung'ani aitalaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok le sile enye. Amu ore Olng'ur le Enkai naa meitalaku ninye nkikilikuanat e Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok. Kaa naa etaasa "Lojos" (Ororei) apa otii te nebo Enkai o laa Enkai? Eitotiwuoki ninye "Olsharunoni" lang, eitotiwuoki olalashe lang too nkiri o osarge pee enya "esipata e Esharunoto", metotiwuo olonyikoki le nkiguana oidim aitalaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok.

Kejo Atanasius:

"Amu ore pee edol "Lojos" (Ororei) nchere meidimayu enkitonoto e ng'ok te pooki sirit e tung'ani aidaikieki te neme te keeya, naa metiu aajo keye Ororei amu meye ninye... neya te kewan enye osesen leinyaanyukie osesen lang... pee eaku olonyikoki le pooki te keeya, neishooyo ninye te Papa Enkai... naa te nkishooroto o sesen lenye anaa Esharunoto te nkaraki osesen le pooki, eitalakua esile apa natigilu iyiook te keeya enye" ("Enkitotio e Ororei" - Inkigurat 8 o 9).

Keitigila Atanasius ene te ng'eno e nkilepunoto nanyori nchere Enkai Ororei "neya te kewan enye osesen leinyaanyukie osesen lang" (etotiwuoki te iyiook Olsharunoni o olalashe) "pee eaku olonyikoki le pooki" neas esiaai e nkinyikokoto nabo "naa te nkishooroto o sesen lenye anaa Esharunoto... eitalakua esile apa natigilu iyiook te keeya enye".

Meetae Enjeunoto nemelakieki olekoisiayio loo ng'ok 5

Keitung'uaiki iyiook Osotua Musana te dukuya esipata e lalahe e Nkai nemeata eripata: kira iyiook isinkan oosilenokino Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok naitame Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok, ilo lemeidim aing'ar esupatisho enye nanyori. Meidimayu enkotunoto metaasi te "Enkitasotata te Kristo te nebo" aashu "Enkibulunoto oo ltau te nkiyang'et" ake anaa enatem kulikae aitiik Esharunoto naitobir Enkai Nanyokie taata. Keyieu enkotunoto "Olsharunoni" oidim naayieu aitashe te wueji ang, neshilaa eilo le sile ang, neitalaku olekoisiayio loo ng'ok pooki te Esupatisho e Enkai naaitame ng'ok. Ena ninye Olng'ur le Enkai o Emayianata nemeata esile karsis nchere «... eitu elotu Olayioni lo Ltung'ani meisiaayiaki kake pee eisiaisho neishooyo enkishui enye aaku Esharunoto te nkaraki kumok» (Marko 10:45).

Ena ninye enkop nagol neshetie Osotua Ng'ejuk elikioroto enye kitok: eewuo Olsharunoni oanyunyekite, kake kaa olekoisiayio loo ng'ok leitalaku ninye? Ena nikidol iyiook tiatua esirata nasuju.

Ndamunoto: eikudukud nkisomata oo rorei tiatua ena sirit pee eisho endamunoto te nkoitoi natii ewang'an. Te nkisomata nadolou indim aitasujaa kulo rorei tiatua Ororei le Nkai ake.
