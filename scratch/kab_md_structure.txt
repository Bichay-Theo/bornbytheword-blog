Line 2: « Ma d nekk ẓriɣ belli Bab n ufdu-inu yedder, yerna ɣer taggara ɣef lqaɛa ad ibedd » (Ayyub 19: 25).
Line 4: « Ad ilin imeslayen n yimi-w d wuxemmem n wul-iw d ayen yelhan zdat-ek a Sidi Ṛebbi, aẓru-inu d Bab n ufdu-inu » (Amezmur 19: 14).
Line 15: ## Lɛahd Aqdim akk-d yimeslayen n usebbel deg wemkan-nneɣ akk-d ufdu
Line 21: ## "Bab n ufdu" (Go'el) akk-d lqanun n ufdu
Line 26: > « Ma yella yefqir gma-k, yezzenz ayla-s, a d-yas bab n ufdu-ines i s-yettilin, ad ifdu ayen yezzenz gma-s ».
Line 33: > « Yenna-d Bab n ufdu: ur zmireɣ ara ad fduɣ i yiman-iw iwakken ur ssexsaṛeɣ ara lwerṭ-inu. Fdu kecč i yiman-ik afdu-inu, axaṭer ur zmireɣ ara ad fduɣ » (Ṛaɛut 4: 6).
Line 35: ## "Lfedya" (Padhah) akk-d lfeṭṭa n usebbel.. I wanwa ara yettwaxelles wazal?
Line 38: > « Mkul amenzu deg warraw-ik a t-tefduḍ... Imi Sidi Ṛebbi yenɣa mkul amenzu di tmurt n Maṣer, seg umenzu n wemdan armi d amenzu n lmal. Ɣef wayagi i zelluɣ i Sidi Ṛebbi... yerna fedduɣ mkul amenzu deg warraw-iw ».
Line 43: > « Ad yefk mkul yiwen lfedya n teṛwiḥt-is i Sidi Ṛebbi... at-tefkem lweɛda n Sidi Ṛebbi i usebbel ɣef teṛwiḥin-nwen ».
Line 47: ## Imezura n teglizt: Yasuɛ d Bab n ufdu n tideț
Line 56: > "Axaṭer mi gwala "Lujus" (Awal) belli axsar amatu n yemدانen ur yezmir ara ad yettwakkes anagar s lmut, yerna d lmuḥal ad immet Wawal axaṭer ur yettmettat ara... yeddem i yiman-is lǧețța ur nxulef ara lǧețța-nneɣ... iwakken ad yuɣal d abdil deg wemkan n meṛṛa di lmut, a t-yefk i Ababat... ihi s usebbel n lǧețța-s d lfedya ɣef lǧețța n meṛṛa, ixelleṣ aṭṭla i gellan fell-aneɣ s lmut-is" ("Tuɣalin n Wawal d aksum" - Ixfawen 8 d 9).
Line 60: ## Ulac leslak mbla lexlaṣ n wazal
Line 65: > «... Mmi-s n bunadem ur d-yusi ara iwakken ad qedcen fell-as wiyaḍ, meɛna d nețța ara iqedcen fell-asen, yerna ad isebbel tudert-is iwakken ad isellek aṭas n yemdanen » (Marqus 10: 45).
